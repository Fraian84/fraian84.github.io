2. Information you enter and store locally

Depending on the features you choose, the App can store the following information in private storage on your Android device:

3. Camera, microphone and device sensors

  • Camera: used when you open camera-based augmented-reality previews or choose to capture a supporting image. Live frames are processed for the feature; snapshots may be saved in the App's private storage. They are not intentionally uploaded to a developer server.
  • Microphone: used only when you activate the sound meter. The App processes audio samples in memory to estimate sound levels. Raw audio is not intended to be saved as a recording or transmitted to the developer; numerical measurement results may be stored locally.
  • Motion and environmental sensors: accelerometer, light sensor and magnetometer readings are processed for level, illuminance and magnetic-field tools. Calibration values or offsets may be saved locally.
  • Biometric authentication: if enabled, Android's biometric framework performs authentication. The App receives the authentication result and does not access or store your fingerprint or face data.

Camera, microphone and overlay permissions are optional for the features that need them. You can deny permissions in Android settings, but the corresponding features may not work.
