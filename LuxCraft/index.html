You do not need to create an account to use LuxCraft. We do not operate our own account system, measurement-history server or cloud service for the App’s measurement records. Most information you enter or generate is stored locally on your device, as described below.

2. Sensor readings and measurement records

While you use the lux meter, the App reads ambient-light sensor values to calculate and display illuminance and related indicators, which may include lux, foot-candles, minimum, maximum, average and uniformity. Live readings are processed on your device and are not designed to be uploaded to a server operated by us.

If you choose to save a measurement, the App may store a record in its local database.
