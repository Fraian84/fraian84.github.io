Privacy Policy

This policy explains how VoltCraft handles information when you use the app. VoltCraft is designed to work primarily on your device and does not operate a developer-owned account system or backend server.

1. Who is responsible

Developer / Data Controller: Fraian — Individual Developer, Italy.

Contact: fraian_dev@protonmail.com.

For privacy requests, please use the contact details above. This page is intended for users internationally; mandatory privacy rights under the laws applicable to you remain unaffected.

2. Information stored locally on your device

Depending on which features you use, VoltCraft may store information that you enter or generate, including:

  • Installer or business details, such as business name, contact person, tax/VAT identifier, address, phone number, email and website.
  • Customer and job-site details, such as names or company names, addresses, tax identifiers, phone numbers, email addresses and notes.
  • Quotes, line items, prices, discounts, taxes, dates, signatures or drawings, price lists, kits and records used to prepare technical documents or declarations.
  • App preferences, language settings, usage counters and security settings.
  • If you enable app protection, a salted hash of your PIN. Biometric authentication is handled by Android; the app receives the authentication result, not your fingerprint or face data.

This information is stored in the app's private storage, including its local database and preferences. The developer does not receive it through a VoltCraft-operated server.

3. Sensors, camera and local network scanning

SensorsMagnetic-field, acceleration, inclination and ambient-light readings are processed while the relevant tool is active. They are not continuously collected in the background. A report may be saved locally if you choose to do so.
Camera / ARCamera frames and motion data are processed for augmented-reality features, including plan measurement and CCTV camera placement. The app does not intentionally record audio or upload camera frames to a developer server.
Network scannerWhen you start a scan, local network information such as IP addresses, hostnames, ports and MAC addresses may be displayed temporarily to help identify devices on your local Wi-Fi network. This information is not sent to a VoltCraft server.
Floating calculatorIf you grant Android's special overlay permission, the floating calculator can appear above other apps. This permission is used to display the calculator and is not intended to capture screen contents.

Camera, network-scanning, sensor and overlay features are optional; denying a permission may disable only the feature that needs it.

4. Advertising and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

The free version may display banner, interstitial and rewarded advertisements provided by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). A rewarded advertisement may be offered to unlock an individual export or a limited one-hour ad-free work pass. Advertisements are disabled during the seven-day trial and while a qualifying VoltCraft Pro entitlement is active.

Google UMP consent management is used to present and manage applicable advertising privacy choices. Depending on your location and applicable requirements, UMP may ask you to consent to, reject or manage the use of data for advertising. You can reopen available privacy choices through the app's advertising privacy settings when that control is available.

AdMob and its service providers may process information such as the device's advertising identifier, IP address, app and device information, ad requests, diagnostics and interactions with ads to deliver, measure, protect and improve advertising, subject to your choices and applicable settings. Google processes this information under its own terms and privacy disclosures.

More information: Google advertising technologies and Google Privacy Policy.

5. Google Play purchases and subscriptions

VoltCraft uses Google Play Billing to manage the monthly Pro subscription, the one-time lifetime purchase and purchase restoration. Google Play handles payment details and order processing. VoltCraft may receive purchase status or purchase tokens from Google Play to determine whether Pro features should be enabled. The developer does not receive your full payment-card details.

See Google Play's terms and information and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Backups, exports and sharing

VoltCraft supports a password-protected .vcbak backup encrypted using AES-256-GCM, with key derivation based on PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256. You control where this exported file is saved or shared. Anyone who obtains the file and its password may be able to access its contents; keep both secure.

Android system backup may also back up eligible app data to the Google account associated with your device, depending on Android settings, system behavior and your backup configuration. This is a platform-managed function, not a VoltCraft-operated cloud service. When you choose to share a PDF or other export, the selected third-party app or service will handle it under its own policies.

7. Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until you delete them, clear VoltCraft's app data, or uninstall the app, subject to Android system backup, files you exported, and copies you shared or saved elsewhere. Clearing app data or uninstalling VoltCraft may remove local records that have not been backed up; the developer cannot recover them because no developer-owned account or backend stores them.

To delete local app data, use Android's app settings. Separately delete exported backups and shared copies from the locations where you saved them. Android-managed backups are governed by your Google account and device backup settings.

8. Security

VoltCraft uses Android's private app storage and offers optional PIN/biometric protection. The manual backup file is encrypted as described above. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure; protect your device, credentials and backup password, and keep independent backups of important business records.

9. Children

VoltCraft is a professional utility intended for adult tradespeople and technical users. It is not designed for children and is not intended to target them.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect app changes, service-provider changes or legal requirements. The latest version will be published at this page with an updated revision date.

Do not rely on VoltCraft as the sole basis for electrical work, drilling, demolition, installation approval or safety-critical decisions.

Calculations and estimates—including voltage drop, conductor sizing, prospective short-circuit current, load balancing, UPS runtime, thermal output and similar results—are provided as preliminary assistance only. They may depend on assumptions, user inputs, local standards and device or installation conditions. They are not a substitute for an appropriately qualified professional's design, independent verification, calibrated instruments or legally required inspections and tests.

TeslaTrace / magnetic cable detection: the feature interprets changes measured by the phone's magnetometer. It is not a certified utility locator and cannot guarantee detection of shielded cables, unenergized conductors, deeply buried services, plastic pipes or other concealed objects. Never rely on it alone before drilling, cutting or demolishing a wall. Use appropriate certified detection equipment and safe work procedures.

Level and lux measurements: accuracy varies with the device's sensor quality, calibration, physical condition, case or magnetic accessories, placement, lighting and surrounding environment. Results are indicative unless independently verified with suitable calibrated equipment.
