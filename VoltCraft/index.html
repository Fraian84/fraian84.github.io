About WaveShift

WaveShift (package name com.waveshift.native) is an Android audio utility for playing, recording, editing, trimming and converting audio files. Audio processing is designed to take place locally on your device. The app does not operate a developer-owned account system, remote media library or backend for storing your audio files.

Developer / Data Controller: Fraian — Individual Developer, Italy
Privacy and support contact: fraian_dev@protonmail.com

1. Privacy Policy

1.1 Scope and principles

This Policy explains how WaveShift handles information. WaveShift is designed as an offline-first application: importing, playing, recording, editing, trimming and converting audio are performed on your device. The developer does not provide a server to receive or store your audio library, recordings or edited projects.

1.2 Audio files and media library

When you choose audio files or grant relevant media access, WaveShift may access supported local formats, including MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG and AAC, to display, play, edit or convert them. The app may also receive files that you deliberately share from another application through Android sharing functions.

  • Selected audio files are processed locally for playback, waveform display, trimming, effects and conversion.
  • Converted or exported files are saved to a destination selected or permitted through Android storage facilities.
  • The app does not intentionally upload imported tracks, recordings or edited audio to the developer.
  • If you share a file with another app or service, that recipient processes it under its own terms and privacy policy.

1.3 Microphone and voice recordings

WaveShift requests microphone access only when you use the audio recorder. Microphone input is used to create the recording you request, with available sample rates and encoding quality depending on your device and selected settings. Recordings are saved locally or to a location you choose. WaveShift does not intentionally transmit microphone input or saved recordings to the developer's servers.

You are responsible for obtaining any consent required before recording other people and for complying with applicable recording, privacy and data-protection laws.

1.4 Editing, effects and conversion

Editing may include gain adjustment, noise suppression, vocal enhancement, fade-in/fade-out and trimming. Processing is performed locally. Temporary working data or cache files may be created and removed through the app's cache-management functions or Android settings. Supported codecs, sample rates, bitrates and channels can vary with the source file, device and output format. Conversion may alter audio quality or metadata.

1.5 Information stored on your device

The app may store settings and operational information needed to provide its functions, such as theme preferences, playback or editing settings, cache information, Free usage counters and purchase-entitlement state. Audio files and recordings remain in local storage or storage locations you select. The developer does not have routine access to this locally stored information.

Local data may be lost if you uninstall the app, clear its data, reset or damage your device, or delete files. Android backup or transfer features may apply according to your device and account settings; those services are governed by their providers' terms.

1.8 Android permissions

  • Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`): Used when you start an audio recording. Audio input is processed locally and never uploaded to remote developer servers.
  • Audio/media access (`READ_MEDIA_AUDIO` or legacy storage access): Used to find and open audio files you choose to play, edit, or convert.
  • Legacy storage write access (where applicable): Used to save exported audio files on older Android versions.
  • Foreground media playback service: Keeps playback running in the background or with the screen off and provides system media controls.
  • Notifications (Android 13+): May display playback controls and active recording or conversion progress status.
  • Vibration: Provides optional tactile haptic feedback.
  • Internet & Network State (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`): Required for Google AdMob ad delivery, UMP consent verification, and Google Play licensing validation; it is not used to upload your personal audio library to a developer server.
  • Advertising ID (`com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID`): Merged automatically by Google Mobile Ads SDK dependencies to allow compliant ad serving, frequency capping, and fraud detection on Android 13+ devices.

Permissions are used strictly for the functions described above. You can manage them in Android settings, although denying a permission may make the related feature unavailable.
