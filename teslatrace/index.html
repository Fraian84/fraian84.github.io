Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Fraian — Individual Developer (“the Developer”, “I” or “me”) handles information in connection with TeslaTrace (“the App”), an Android application that uses the device's magnetic-field sensor to help users investigate magnetic-field variations, locate certain metal objects and possible electrical-cable paths, save readings and create technical reports.

At a glance: TeslaTrace does not require an account and has no Developer-operated server or cloud account. Scanning, saved measurements, company-profile information and report generation are processed on your device. Google AdMob/UMP and Google Play Billing may process information through their own SDKs and services, as explained below.

1. Developer and contact

The App is provided by Fraian — Individual Developer, based in Italy. For privacy questions or requests concerning processing for which the Developer is responsible, contact fraian_dev@protonmail.com.

2. Information processed and stored on your device

The App does not require registration or a remote user account. During scanning, the App processes magnetic-field sensor readings in memory on the device. These readings may include magnetic-field values, waveform information, estimated depth, alert indicators and scan-mode information. Live readings are not sent to the Developer and are not saved in history unless you choose to save a measurement.

  • Saved measurement records: a saved record may contain a timestamp, label supplied by you, peak or maximum reading, alert level, scan mode, waveform data, estimated depth, technical/normative reference and country selection. These records are saved in the App on your device.
  • Company/report profile: if you use the optional company profile, information you enter may include a company name, address, tax/VAT identifier, telephone number, email, certified email or electronic-invoicing details, and report-branding preferences. This information is stored locally and may be inserted into PDFs you create.
  • Language and country preferences: selected language, country, unit preferences and related settings are stored locally.
  • Local entitlement and trial state: the App stores information such as the first-launch timestamp, trial status, local scan counters and purchase-entitlement state to operate the introductory trial, enforce Free limits and reflect Google Play purchase status. The trial is managed locally and is separate from a Google Play subscription offer.

App data is stored locally on your device. As with any app, you should protect access to your device and keep it updated.

3. Sensors, permissions and technical processing

The App requests and utilizes system permissions strictly necessary for its technical operation:

  • Magnetic-Field Sensor: Processed directly in device memory to analyze magnetic variations and estimate cable or metal presence without uploading sensor telemetry to external servers;
  • Vibration (`android.permission.VIBRATE`): Used optionally to provide tactile haptic feedback when detecting threshold magnetic spikes;
  • Internet & Network State (`android.permission.INTERNET`, `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`): Required for Google AdMob ad delivery, UMP consent verification, downloadable fonts, and Google Play licensing validation;
  • Advertising ID (`com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID`): Merged automatically by Google Mobile Ads SDK dependencies to allow compliant ad serving, frequency capping, and fraud detection on Android 13+ devices.

The App does not require camera, microphone, device contacts, or GPS location permissions for its scanning features.

PDF reports are generated directly on the device. When you choose to share a report or text summary, Android passes the selected file or text to the application or destination you choose (e.g., email or messaging app). The receiving service's own terms and privacy practices then apply.
