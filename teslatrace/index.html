Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Fraian — Individual Developer (“the Developer”, “I” or “me”) handles information in connection with TeslaTrace (“the App”), an Android application that uses the device's magnetic-field sensor to help users investigate magnetic-field variations, locate certain metal objects and possible electrical-cable paths, save readings and create technical reports.

At a glance: TeslaTrace does not require an account and has no Developer-operated server or cloud account. Scanning, saved measurements, company-profile information and report generation are processed on your device. Google AdMob/UMP and Google Play Billing may process information through their own SDKs and services, as explained below.

1. Developer and contact

The App is provided by Fraian — Individual Developer, based in Italy. For privacy questions or requests concerning processing for which the Developer is responsible, contact fraian_dev@protonmail.com.

2. Information processed and stored on your device

The App does not require registration or a remote user account. During scanning, the App processes magnetic-field sensor readings in memory on the device. These readings may include magnetic-field values, waveform information, estimated depth, alert indicators and scan-mode information. Live readings are not sent to the Developer and are not saved in history unless you choose to save a measurement.

  • Saved measurement records: a saved record may contain a timestamp, label supplied by you, peak or maximum reading, alert level, scan mode, waveform data, estimated depth, technical/normative reference and country selection. These records are saved in the App on your device.
  • Company/report profile: if you use the optional company profile, information you enter may include a company name, address, tax/VAT identifier, telephone number, email, certified email or electronic-invoicing details, and report-branding preferences. This information is stored locally and may be inserted into PDFs you create.
  • Language and country preferences: selected language, country, unit preferences and related settings are stored locally.
  • Local entitlement and trial state: the App stores information such as the first-launch timestamp, trial status, local scan counters and purchase-entitlement state to operate the introductory trial, enforce Free limits and reflect Google Play purchase status. The trial is managed locally and is separate from a Google Play subscription offer.

App data is stored locally on your device. As with any app, you should protect access to your device and keep it updated.

3. Sensors, permissions and technical processing

The App uses the device's magnetic-field sensor to read magnetic-field changes. It does not require camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location permissions for its scanning features. Vibration may be used to provide haptic feedback. Internet access is used by third-party services for advertising, privacy choices, fonts and purchase processing. Scanning and report creation take place on your device.

PDF reports are generated on the device. When you choose to share a report or text summary, Android passes the selected content to the application or destination you choose, such as an email or messaging app. The receiving app's own privacy practices then apply.

The App includes an introductory trial lasting seven days from the first launch. The trial is managed on your device and is separate from any offer available through Google Play.

8. Android backup, device transfer and manual encrypted backups

Android system backup and device-transfer rules may include the App's measurement database and selected company-profile and international-preference files. The local billing/trial preferences and temporary backup-staging directory are excluded from the configured Android automatic backup rules. Actual backup and transfer behavior depends on Android version, device settings and the Google backup service.

Android may back up some App data or transfer it to another device, depending on your device and backup settings. Separately, the App offers an optional password-protected manual backup in a .ttbak file. You choose where to save the file. The App does not send it to the Developer. If you save it using a cloud-storage service, that service's own terms and privacy settings apply.

The Developer cannot recover a forgotten backup password, so keep it safe. Delete backup files from the place where you saved them when you no longer need them. PDF reports and shared text may contain readings, labels, timestamps, technical references and any company details you have chosen to include.

9. Retention and deletion

  • Saved scans: open the App's History screen and use the available delete action for an individual record.
  • Company/report profile: edit or clear the information in Settings / Company Profile.
  • Language and country preferences: change them in Settings where the relevant controls are available.
  • All local App data: use Android Settings → Apps → TeslaTrace → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the App. Menu names vary by device and Android version.
  • Exported reports and backups: delete PDF or .ttbak files separately from the destination where you saved them.

Deleting information inside the App does not necessarily delete files already exported, copies shared with other apps, or eligible copies in Android system backups. Information processed by Google for advertising or purchases must be managed through the relevant Google privacy, account or device controls where available.

No software, device storage, encryption or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect access to your device and keep backup passwords confidential. The Developer cannot remotely erase data stored only on your device.
