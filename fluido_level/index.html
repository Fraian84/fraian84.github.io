Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to FluidoLevel (the “App”), a digital spirit level that uses your device’s built-in accelerometer to display inclination and slope. It is provided by Fraian, an individual developer based in Italy (“we”, “us” or “our”).

1. Information handled by the App

FluidoLevel does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to submit your name, email address or other personal details to use its measuring features.

The App processes accelerometer readings on your device to calculate inclination, slope and related indicators while you use it. The App’s measuring code is not designed to upload these readings to our own server, and the App does not provide a cloud measurement-history feature.

2. Settings stored on your device

The App stores certain preferences locally so they can be retained between uses. These may include:

  • your target slope;
  • calibration offsets and calibration status for supported device orientations;
  • whether sound feedback is enabled; and
  • whether vibration feedback is enabled.

These settings are stored using Android’s local application preferences. The App does not provide a feature to send them to our own server. Their handling during device backup, device migration or restoration can also depend on the Android version, device manufacturer and system configuration. Uninstalling the App normally removes its local application data, subject to Android and device-specific behavior.

When advertising services are used, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP address, app and device information, advertising identifiers where available, approximate location inferred from IP address, and information about ad delivery or interaction. The information processed and the purposes for which it is used depend on the services involved, your device settings, applicable requirements and the advertising configuration in effect.

The App integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to support consent information and privacy choices where required. Depending on your location, applicable requirements and the consent status returned by Google's services, the App may display a consent message or provide access to a privacy options form. The availability and content of these messages depend on the applicable configuration and Google's services. You can manage the privacy settings available to you through the App and your device, as applicable.

We do not operate the advertising network ourselves and do not control all data processing carried out by Google or its partners. For more information, consult Google’s Privacy Policy, the Google AdMob documentation and the Google UMP privacy documentation.

The App may offer an optional rewarded video that temporarily disables ads for one hour after the reward is successfully granted. This feature is optional; you can continue using the App without choosing the reward. Reward availability may depend on the advertising service, network connectivity, region and other factors.

Google Play processes the transaction and manages the purchase through the relevant Google Play account. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details. The App uses the device’s accelerometer for measurement and may use vibration functionality when enabled. It also requires network access for advertising and related network operations. The Android permissions declared by the current app build include Internet access, network-state access and vibration. The App does not need camera or microphone access for its core measuring function.

6. Data retention and security

Preferences stored by the App remain on the device unless removed by the user, the operating system or an app-data reset/uninstall. We do not maintain a server-side account or measurement-history database for these features. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
