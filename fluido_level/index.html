Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to FluidoLevel (the “App”), a digital spirit level that uses your device’s built-in accelerometer to display inclination and slope. It is provided by Fraian, an individual developer based in Italy (“we”, “us” or “our”).

1. Information handled by the App

FluidoLevel does not require user account registration and does not ask for or collect your name, email address, physical address or direct contact details to use its measuring features.

The App processes hardware sensor readings (accelerometer) directly on your device in real time to calculate inclination, pitch, roll, slope and visual indicators. The App’s core measurement processing is purely local; sensor readings are not transmitted, uploaded, or synced to any remote server or cloud database operated by us.

2. Settings stored on your device

The App stores certain operational preferences locally on your device via standard Android application storage so they persist between sessions. These include:

  • Target slope values and custom angle thresholds;
  • Sensor calibration offsets and status across supported device orientations;
  • Haptic and sound feedback preferences; and
  • User interface presentation choices.

These settings remain stored locally on your device. We do not operate remote servers to synchronise or store these values. Their inclusion in operating system device backups depends entirely on your Android version, device manufacturer, and OS-level backup configurations. Clearing application data or uninstalling the App will remove these locally stored settings in accordance with Android operating system behavior.

When loading and displaying advertisements, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process certain device and usage data. Depending on your region, operating system version, and consent choices, this data may include:

  • Advertising Identifiers: The Google Advertising ID (GAID / AD_ID) provided by Google Play Services;
  • Network & Device Information: Approximate location inferred from coarse IP address, mobile network state, hardware model, operating system version, screen resolution, and system language;
  • Diagnostic & Performance Data: Ad delivery performance, interaction metrics, click data, and crash/diagnostic logs.

Purposes of Advertising Data Processing:

  • Delivery & Serving: Technical rendering and loading of ad creative assets on your device screen;
  • Frequency Capping: Limiting the repetitive display of the same ad to maintain user experience;
  • Performance Measurement: Auditing and reporting ad impressions, engagement, and conversion metrics;
  • Fraud Prevention & Security: Detecting bot activity, invalid clicks, and abusive or fraudulent traffic patterns;
  • Personalization: Serving ads tailored to inferred user interests, only where permitted by applicable regulations and when valid affirmative consent has been granted by the user.

User Control Over Advertising Identifiers:
You can control, reset, or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time via your device settings (typically located under Settings > Google > Ads or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device manufacturer and Android version). Deleting or resetting the identifier will disassociate future advertising requests from previous ad tracking profiles.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) & Consent Management:
The App incorporates the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK as part of its standard release configuration to facilitate consent gathering and regulatory compliance across the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland under the GDPR and ePrivacy frameworks. 5. Permissions and Device Sensors

FluidoLevel requires minimal system permissions strictly tied to its functionality:

  • Accelerometer / Motion Sensors: Used in real time for measuring pitch, roll, and angle. Sensor values are processed in memory and never saved to a remote server.
  • Vibration (`android.permission.VIBRATE`): Used optionally to provide tactile haptic feedback when reaching target levels or specific angle thresholds.
  • Internet & Network State (`android.permission.INTERNET`, `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`): Required for Google AdMob ad delivery, UMP consent verification, and Google Play licensing validation.
  • Advertising ID (`com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID`): Merged automatically by Google Mobile Ads SDK dependencies to allow compliant ad serving and fraud detection on Android 13+ devices.

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, device contacts, location services (GPS), or shared storage.

6. Data Retention and Security

Local preferences and calibration offsets remain on your device until manually reset by you, cleared via Android Application Settings, or removed through uninstallation. Because we do not transmit or store your personal data on external servers, retention of local data is under your direct control. Transmission of ad-related telemetry to Google is secured by standard industry encryption (HTTPS/TLS) managed by Google's client libraries.

7. Children’s Privacy

FluidoLevel is a general-purpose utility application designed for DIY and tool measurement purposes. It is not directed at or designed to attract children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in applicable EU jurisdictions). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children.

8. Your Choices and Privacy Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

You may also consult Google's official privacy tools to exercise rights of access, rectification, or deletion regarding Google accounts and advertising profiles.
