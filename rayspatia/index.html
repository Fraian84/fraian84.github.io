Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to RaySpatia (the “App”), a mobile tool for augmented-reality measurements, floor plans, 2D/3D project editing, technical documentation and supported Bluetooth laser distance meters. The App is provided by Fraian — Individual Developer, based in Italy (“we”, “us” or “our”).

1. No account required

RaySpatia does not require you to create an account or sign in to an account operated by the developer. The App does not provide a developer-operated cloud account or project synchronisation service. Most project information is stored locally on your device.

2. Camera, augmented reality and device sensors

When you use AR measurement features, the App accesses the camera and processes camera frames and device motion/sensor information to estimate planes, points, distances and spatial geometry. This processing is used to operate the measurement features. According to the audited app architecture, camera frames and AR processing data are handled on the device and are not intentionally uploaded to a server operated by the developer.

The App does not provide a cloud service for storing or reviewing your camera feed or AR measurements. Third-party platform components needed to run AR functionality may operate under their own technical and privacy terms; see the third-party services section below.

3. Projects, measurements and site photos

Project data may include vertex coordinates, lengths, heights, room dimensions, floor-plan geometry, calculated areas or volumes, project labels and related technical notes. These records are stored in the App’s local Room database on your device.

If you choose to take site photos and attach them to a project, those images are stored in the App’s private local storage. Project data and photos are not sent to the developer’s servers by the App’s normal local-storage workflow. They may leave your device if you explicitly export or share a file using Android’s sharing features or another destination you choose.

4. Company and professional details

If you use the company-profile feature, you may enter information such as business name, VAT/tax number, address, telephone number, email address, certified email (PEC), recipient code (SDI), or other details for a technical document’s title block. These details are stored locally in the App’s preferences and may be included in documents you generate. You are responsible for ensuring that you have a lawful basis to enter and share any personal or business information concerning other people.

5. Bluetooth laser devices and location permission

If you choose to connect a compatible Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) laser distance meter, the App may process the device name, Bluetooth identifier/address and measurement values needed to find and communicate with the device. This is optional and is used for the connection and measurement function.

On some Android versions, the operating system requires location permission for legacy Bluetooth scanning APIs. The App may request this permission for that compatibility purpose. The audited implementation declares the Bluetooth scan permission with a “never for location” flag on supported Android versions. The App is not designed to collect or track your GPS coordinates as part of the BLE laser connection feature.

6. Advertising and Google services

The free version of RaySpatia may display App Open, banner, interstitial, native and rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as your IP address, advertising identifier where available, device and app information, ad delivery and interaction information, and diagnostic or network data to provide and measure advertising, prevent abuse and operate their services. The information processed may depend on your region, device settings, applicable consent choices and Google’s current service configuration.

RaySpatia uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present and manage privacy and advertising-consent choices where required. Consent information and the choices made by the user are handled through Google’s consent-management services and are used to determine the advertising options available in the app, in accordance with the applicable regional requirements and the current AdMob configuration. Users can review or change available privacy choices through the consent options provided by the app, where supported.

More information is available in Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising technologies information.

7. Google Play Billing and purchases

RaySpatia offers, where available through Google Play, a monthly Pro subscription and a one-time lifetime purchase. Google Play handles payment processing, purchase records, account association, subscription renewal and cancellation, refunds and purchase validation under its own terms. The App may store local purchase or entitlement state and purchase tokens or related identifiers required by Google Play Billing. The developer does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

The price, currency, taxes, renewal terms and availability shown by Google Play at checkout are the ones that apply to your transaction. For details, consult the Google Play Terms of Service and Google’s payments privacy notice.

8. ARCore, downloadable fonts and other third-party components

The App uses Google ARCore-related technology through its AR library for augmented-reality functionality. Camera and sensor data are processed to provide the AR experience. The App may also request downloadable fonts through Google Play services; when fonts are downloaded, the relevant service may receive technical connection information such as an IP address. These services are provided by third parties and may be governed by their own privacy terms. See the Google AR privacy information and Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

The audited project did not identify Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. This statement describes the inspected project configuration and is not a guarantee about future versions or third-party platform services.

9. Local storage, backup, sharing and deletion

Project data is stored in the local database, while settings, billing state and company-profile details are stored in local application preferences. You can delete individual projects or clear the project database using the App’s available controls. The App also provides an encrypted archive backup and restore feature for supported users. Backup files are exported to a destination you select; after export, their storage, access and deletion are controlled by you and by the service or device where you place them.

Uninstalling the App or clearing its data through Android normally removes its local application data, but it may not remove copies you exported, shared, or included in Android or other device backups. The developer cannot restore lost local projects if no usable backup exists.

10. Data security and retention

Local app data is retained on your device until you delete it, clear the App’s data, uninstall the App, or the operating system removes it. We do not operate a server-side project database. No storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure; protect your device and any exported backup files, including any password used to protect an archive.

11. Professional use and data entered by you

You control the project details, photos, company information and files you create or share. Do not enter or disclose information unless you are authorised to do so. If you include personal data about clients, occupants, contractors or other individuals in project records or documents, you are responsible for complying with the privacy obligations that apply to your use of that information.

12. Children’s privacy

RaySpatia is a technical and professional measurement tool and is not intended for children. It does not provide child-directed features.

13. Your choices and privacy rights

You can choose whether to use optional features such as site photos, Bluetooth laser connection, company-profile details and file sharing. You can manage or delete local information through the App and Android settings. Depending on your country, you may have rights concerning personal data processed by third-party service providers. Requests about data processed directly by Google may need to be directed to Google.
