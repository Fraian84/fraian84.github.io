Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Fraian — Individual Developer (“the Developer”, “we” or “us”) handles information in connection with SoundMeter (“the App”), an Android application for measuring and analysing sound levels using the microphone and hardware of the device. Features include displaying sound-level readings and trends, saving measurement data, showing country-profile reference information, calibrating the microphone reading by an adjustment value, and exporting reports and data.

Important: The App is designed to process measurement data on your device. Advertising, Google Play purchases, Android backup and other platform services may separately process information as described below.

1. Developer and contact

The App is provided by Fraian — Individual Developer. For privacy questions or requests, contact fraian_dev@protonmail.com.

2. Information stored locally by the App

The App does not require you to create an account and does not provide a Developer-operated account or cloud-sync service. The following information may be stored locally on your device:

  • Measurement records: numerical sound readings and related statistics, including current, minimum, maximum, average and peak values, together with time-series samples captured during a measurement.
  • Company/report details: details you enter to customise exported reports, which may include a company name, tax or VAT number, address, telephone number, email address and other business contact fields.
  • App settings: selected country or regulatory profile, language-related preferences where applicable, calibration offset, filters, theme and other configuration choices.
  • Purchase-related state: information needed locally to reflect the status of Google Play purchases or entitlements. Purchases themselves are processed by Google Play.

The App uses the microphone to sample audio in real time for sound-level calculations. Audio samples are processed in memory while measuring; the App is designed not to save or transmit microphone audio as a WAV, MP3 or other audio recording. The App saves numerical measurement results rather than a replayable audio recording.

Information stored locally is generally accessible to the App on your device. The Developer does not receive it merely because it is saved in the App. If you choose to export a PDF, CSV or backup file, that file is saved to the destination you select and may contain personal or business information you entered.

3. Microphone and Android permissions

Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): used to sample sound in real time and calculate sound-level readings. The App is not designed to retain the sampled audio as an audio recording.

Internet and network state: used by integrated services such as advertising and Google Play Billing to connect and determine network availability.

File/export access: depending on Android version and the export route, legacy storage permissions may be declared for saving PDF, CSV or backup files. Where Android’s Storage Access Framework is used, you select the destination for the exported file.

7. Android system backup and device transfer

The App enables Android’s system backup and provides backup rules that include certain app data, currently described as the SoundMeter database and selected company/international preference files, while excluding specified staging and billing-preference files. Depending on Android version, device settings, backup transport and system behavior, eligible app data may be included in Google-managed cloud backup and/or device-to-device transfer.

Android system backup is separate from the App’s manual encrypted backup feature. Availability, encryption, retention and restoration of system backups are governed by Android, Google services, your device and your backup settings. Deleting data from the App or uninstalling it does not necessarily immediately remove an existing system backup or a transferred copy. Review the backup controls in your Android and Google account settings for available options.

8. Manual encrypted backups and exported files

The App’s manual backup feature creates a .smbak file that can contain structured measurement history, company profile information, settings and calibration preferences. It does not contain microphone audio recordings. The backup is encrypted using AES-256-GCM, with a random initialisation vector and a key derived from the password you choose using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256 and a random salt.

The backup is saved to a destination you select, which may include device storage, removable storage or a location made available by a file-provider app such as Google Drive. The Developer does not know or recover your backup password. If you lose that password, the Developer may be unable to restore the backup. Once exported, the file is under your control and the storage provider’s policies; delete it separately if you no longer want it retained.

PDF and CSV exports may contain measurement results and any company details included in the report. The App does not automatically delete files you have exported. You are responsible for managing and deleting exported files from the destination where you saved them.

9. Retention and deletion

  • Individual measurement: open History, use the measurement’s menu and choose Delete, then confirm.
  • Company details: edit or clear the information in Company Profile/Settings and restore neutral defaults where offered.
  • Calibration: reset the calibration offset in the Calibration & Filters dialog.
  • All local App data: use Android Settings → Apps → SoundMeter → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the App. Menu names may differ by device and Android version.
  • Exports and backups: manually delete any PDF, CSV or .smbak files from the destination where you saved them.

These actions affect the data stored locally by the App as described, but may not delete files already exported, data held by Google or other service providers, or eligible copies in Android system backups. Those items may need to be managed separately through the relevant provider or device settings.

10. Security

The App uses local Android storage for its records and encrypts manual backup files with the method described above. No method of storage, encryption, transmission or software operation can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect access to your device and keep manual-backup passwords safe.
